Who We Are

A community for first-generation students

Tri-Alpha is an academic honor society that recognizes the achievements of first-generation college students.

Our chapter at Colorado School of Mines brings first-generation students together through community events, mentorship, leadership, service, and professional development.

We are here to celebrate our members’ accomplishments, help students build meaningful connections, and make sure first-generation students know they belong at Mines.

Our Mission

Recognize. Connect. Support.

We celebrate the strength and determination of first-generation students while creating opportunities for them to connect, lead, serve, and succeed.
